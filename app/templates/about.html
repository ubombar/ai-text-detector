About

This tool runs four detectors locally and combines them into a verdict. No text leaves your machine.

Methods

Binoculars

Ratio of the text's log-perplexity under the instruct model to the cross-perplexity between the base and instruct models. Low = AI.

Fast-DetectGPT

Analytic conditional probability curvature, expressed as a z-score: how much more likely the observed tokens are than alternatives sampled from the model. High = AI.

DeBERTa classifier

Supervised classifier desklib/ai-text-detector-v1.01 outputting P(AI). Long texts are chunked and the chunk scores aggregated. High = AI.

GLTR

Each token is ranked by GPT-2's prediction. Buckets: top 10 (green), top 100 (yellow), top 1000 (red), rest (purple). Machine text is dominated by green. GLTR is display only and does not vote.

Voting

Binoculars, Fast-DetectGPT and the classifier each vote. The overall verdict is AI or human when at least 2 of 3 agree; otherwise it is inconclusive.

Metrics

  • AUROC — threshold-independent separability.
  • TPR @ 1% FPR — the most important: how much AI text is caught while wrongly flagging only 1% of humans.
  • FPR — rate of human text flagged as AI.
  • Calibration — whether scores/thresholds match observed rates on your own data.

Calibration

Thresholds can be re-fit on a folder of known human-written texts:

uv run python -m app.calibrate path/to/human_texts/

Models

  • Qwen2.5-3B + Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct (shared by Binoculars and Fast-DetectGPT)
  • DeBERTa-v3 classifier (desklib/ai-text-detector-v1.01)
  • GPT-2 (GLTR)

Caveats

  • Scores are evidence, not proof. Never use them as the sole basis for an accusation.
  • Detectors produce more false positives on non-native English writers (Liang et al., 2023).
  • Paraphrasing or light editing can evade detection.
  • Texts under 250 words give low-confidence results; under 100 tokens are refused.
